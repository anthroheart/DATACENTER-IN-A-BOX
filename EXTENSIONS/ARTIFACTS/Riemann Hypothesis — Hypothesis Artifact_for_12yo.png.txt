THE BASIC QUESTION

Mathematicians study a special mathematical function called the Riemann zeta function.

A function is a rule that takes an input and gives an output.

Some inputs make this function give an answer of zero. These inputs are called its zeros.

The Riemann Hypothesis says that all its nontrivial zeros—the zeros involved in this puzzle—lie on one special line.

The question explored in this infographic is:

“Could a deeper mathematical structure explain why the zeros would have to lie on that line?”
